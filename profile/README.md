## Overview
Micros Oracle Simphony — Oracle's branding for its Simphony point-of-sale platform — brings order entry, payment processing, kitchen coordination, and back-office reporting together in one system. The Simphony app runs on Windows-based POS terminals at the counter, with management and reporting tools reachable through a web browser for owners and managers overseeing one site or many.

## Why Choose Micros Simphony
| Without Micros Simphony | With Micros Simphony |
|---|---|
| Separate systems for orders, payments, and reporting | One platform covering order taking, payments, and reporting together |
| Manual coordination between kitchen and front of house | Built-in kitchen display integration for order routing |
| Limited visibility across multiple locations | Centralized, enterprise-wide reporting for multi-site operations |

## Features

## System Requirements
- **OS:** Windows, running on Oracle-certified POS terminals
- **Processor:** As specified for the certified POS hardware
- **Memory:** As specified for the certified POS hardware
- **Storage:** Sized to the property's order and reporting volume

## Installation
1. Contact Oracle Hospitality or an authorized partner to set up the Micros Simphony app for your location.
2. POS terminals and back-office access are configured as part of that setup.
3. Staff sign in at the terminal, while managers access reporting through a browser.

## Getting Started
Front-of-house staff begin with basic order-entry and payment training on the terminal, while managers set up menus, pricing, and user roles from the back office. From there, daily operations run through taking orders, tracking the kitchen queue, and reviewing sales reports.
